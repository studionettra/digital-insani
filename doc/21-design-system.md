# Design System — digital.insani.id

## Brand

Until a dedicated brand is approved, use Insani Indonesia’s visual
identity as the base, adapted for a digital marketplace.

## Color direction

- Blue = primary/dominant, associated with
  responsibility/professionalism.
- Green = selective secondary accent, such as buy action, bestseller
  badge, or success state.
- Background = neutral white/light gray; optional neutral dark mode.
- Centralize colors using CSS variables/Tailwind theme configuration.

Do not hardcode colors throughout components.

## Typography

Use one modern humanist sans-serif family such as Inter or Manrope.

Differentiate hierarchy through: - font size; - weight; - spacing; not
multiple unrelated font families.

## Layout

Storefront should resemble a functional marketplace: - catalog grid; -
filters; - clear product previews; - strong product information
hierarchy; - obvious purchase CTA.

Do not copy the donation site’s campaign layout.

## Product cards

Featured/bestseller products may use: - stronger border; - badge; -
different hierarchy.

Do not make every card visually identical.

## Avoid AI-slop patterns

Avoid: - warm cream + serif + terracotta; - black + neon accent; -
identical rounded cards everywhere; - excessive soft shadows; - ALL CAPS
labels on every heading; - decorative arrows on every button; -
fade/slide animation everywhere.

## Admin

Filament: - primary color aligned with brand blue; - minimal
customization; - prioritize functionality.
